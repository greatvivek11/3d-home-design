# Approved Design Brief and Open Questions

Approved by the user on 17 October 2026 after a requirements interview. This approves the **brief**, not a site, floor plan, cost estimate or engineering design. [house.json](../house-spec/house.json) is its structured counterpart; [room-schedule.md](room-schedule.md) details the space program.

## Purpose and household

- Fully finished Blender/Three.js dream-home visualization of a future independent family home, potentially near Dehradun or a similar quiet, cooler, mountain/nature-connected setting.
- The user is currently unmarried and living with parents. Plan for a couple, 2-3 children and occasional visiting parents/guests, not an existing five-person household.
- Desired experiences: peace, privacy, generous greenery/open space, daylight, thermal comfort and feeling on vacation at home.
- Avoid the current home's lack of personal space, poor room daylight/ventilation and summer/winter discomfort.
- The future quiet office supports regular work from home. No resident staff room.

## Site and outdoor allocation

- No plot has been purchased. Initial target is 4,000-5,000 sq ft; 500 sq yd means 4,500 sq ft, not 5,000. Flexible upward rather than compressing rooms and gardens.
- Rough targets: front yard 20%, backyard 30% of total plot area. These INCLUDE associated play/planting, paths, driveway/ramp and other uses, not planting alone.
- Side passages connect both yards. Side paths and all access must be explicitly accounted for without double-counting outdoor area.
- Do not derive a fixed 50% house footprint from these targets. Setbacks, side circulation, basement openings/access and other constraints remain unresolved.
- A flat standalone site with a road at the designated front may be used as a labeled study assumption, not an actual site fact. Front does not establish north.
- Amenities within around 30 minutes and schools preferably within 15 minutes are future search preferences, not constraints to solve in this visualization.

## Approved spatial program

### Ground floor and privacy

- Master suite and additional guest bedroom downstairs; three children's bedrooms upstairs: **five bedrooms total**. Office is separate and cannot silently substitute for one.
- Front drawing/receiving zone linked to the house but without direct sightlines into family living/kitchen. Prefer spatial offsets and soft screens/curtains to a fully closed-off plan.
- Front tea/breakfast/guest terrace; family living and relocated kitchen face the backyard, with dining inward beside the kitchen. Children should reach backyard without crossing master bedroom or office.
- Fully open vegetarian Indian kitchen with effective stove extraction, island/breakfast counter, refrigerator, dishwasher, induction and LPG cooking. Pantry room; eight-seat dining nearby.
- Covered verandas are required for rain/shade, with a swing facing backyard. Large operable garden-facing glazing where comfort/privacy permit.
- Master: king bed, reading/seating corner, private direct garden access, closable walk-in wardrobe for two. Shared spacious bath/shower area with bathtub, shower, two basins and **two separate toilet cubicles**.
- Guest: queen bed, reading/seating and freestanding wooden wardrobe, not a walk-in closet. One bathroom with access from both guest bedroom and receiving zone; privacy/locking arrangement requires explicit design.
- Additional family common toilet near living; no need to enter a private bedroom to use it.
- Open prayer alcove beside living, floor seating for 5-10 using living-space overflow, daily diya. No separate enclosed prayer room or screening requested.
- User revision: move kitchen to face the backyard, relocate pantry directly beside it and expand living. Dining may shrink while retaining eight seats and usable clearances; kitchen windows supplement, not replace, ducted source extraction.

### First floor and office

- Three equal children's sleeping rooms, enlarged relative to Study 002, each with queen bed, reading/seating and desk. Each now has a separate private walk-in dressing area directly connected to its shower bathroom; bathing/dressing does not require returning through the bedroom. No children's bathtubs. Equal sleeping rooms do not require identical bath/dressing sizes.
- Soft bath/dressing dividers are allowed, but enclosed showers, extraction and moisture protection are still required. Private suites must not become routes between common spaces.
- Shared seamless upstairs lounge/gallery. Front and rear balconies are each continuous family-shared decks without internal privacy dividers; retain perimeter guarding. Keep common rear-balcony access without crossing bedrooms; the front may be entered from Child 1 or office.
- Quiet closable office swaps with the former front Child 2 room; Child 2 moves to the rear beside Child 3. Retain dedicated PC/monitor workstation PLUS separate rectangular study table for 2-3, bookshelf and digital piano. Daylight/front-garden view; first floor is now selected, superseding the earlier downstairs preference. Not the basement gaming cave; not intended for normal visitor use.
- User selected a private indoor office WC with basin and office-only access, not a WC in the lounge or on a balcony. Total toilets increase from eight to **nine**; no existing toilet is removed.
- Proper fall protection for balconies/windows; insect screens and curtains serve different purposes and are not substitutes for guarding.

### Basement

- Required for independently accessible parking for three SUVs, two motorbikes, one scooter, children's bicycles and EV charging. A visible ramp at one side of the front garden is acceptable.
- Basic gym: treadmill, cycle, weights/rack and yoga space. Gaming couch/TV/consoles plus movable table tennis and foosball. No full commercial gym.
- Revised direction: parking by the ramp's rear basement landing; gym/gaming toward the front, with storage between where feasible. User permits basement enlargement if needed to retain the program and garage separation.
- Occupied recreation, food stores and stairs need clean-side access without crossing the garage. Full-height sealed garage separation, a protected two-door connection and independent ventilation are design intents, not a safety certification; glass or exhaust fans alone do not establish protection.
- Furnish gym/gaming; utilitarian hard-floor finishes elsewhere. Natural light/fresh air desirable; mechanical ventilation and artificial light accepted. Typical recreation visits 2-4 hours, not permission to relax safety requirements.
- Six-month food/grain storage, luggage/tools and emergency supplies; water, electrical and backup equipment locations require review.
- Basement toilet, internal stair connection and outside maintenance access without passing through family interiors. Car washing desirable only if drainage/feasibility supports it. No workbench required.
- Use the smallest workable footprint for this program. The suggested 70-80% plot coverage was withdrawn as a requirement.

### Gardens, roof and utilities

- Front formal garden/lawn for morning tea/breakfast and receiving visitors.
- Backyard play lawn, shrubs, trees/mini-forest edge, private seating and veranda swing. No vegetable garden.
- Small pool for family cooling, relaxing and children's play, not lap swimming; requested maximum depth 4 ft, exact dimensions/profile unresolved. Appropriate barriers/controlled access remain necessary regardless of depth or cover. Cover desired.
- Fishpond optional and lower priority than pool, play space and trees.
- Accessible roof terrace via stairs: pots/planters, shaded seating, gatherings and solar; no extensive soil/lawn roof requirement.
- Ground-floor laundry with practical discreet outdoor drying preferred; basement fallback. Cleaning supplies must not share food storage.
- Induction and LPG cooking, solar/backup power, rainwater collection and water filtration desired. Equipment quantities/locations unapproved.
- No spare vehicle fuel storage. LPG, generator/diesel and battery/electrical equipment must have appropriate specialist-reviewed locations, not general basement storage.
- Hired garden/pool care, automation and service frequency as needed; fortnightly visits are not a fixed maintenance limit.

## Comfort, appearance and access

- Warm, nature-connected modern minimalism: warm white, wood/oak tones, light stone, restrained dark accents; comfortable rather than flashy. No specific reference house.
- Shaded summers, sunny/warm winters, cross-breeze, quiet, daylight and reduced dust/pollen/insects.
- Insulation, appropriate glazing/shading and insect screens; allow less glazing where orientation, comfort or privacy requires. Curtains plus thoughtful layout/planting protect bedroom privacy.
- AC/heating allowed when needed. Do not promise passive comfort, no HVAC, or climate performance from renders.
- Stairs only, no lift. Step-free ground-floor entry/guest access where feasible. Internal stairs connect basement, ground, first and roof; exact circulation and safe egress unapproved.
- Modest double-height stair/atrium optional, not full double-height living. May be cut.
- Vastu desirable where practical, not mandatory; no specific rules supplied. Comfort/daylight/privacy drive the brief.

- Double height and premium finishes are first to defer/cut. Do not silently cut protected functions to make a diagram fit.
- Land target starts at INR 50 lakh, not a hard ceiling.
