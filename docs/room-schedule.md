## Review requirements

Confirm actual furniture sizes and clearances before accepting room dimensions. Test simultaneous activities, drawing-to-family sightlines, bedroom privacy, piano/TV noise, wet-area access, pantry/chemical separation, stair/ramp connectivity and meaningful garden/play area after all access is accounted for.

Professional review is required for structure, code, fire/egress, ventilation, garage exhaust separation, fuel/LPG/batteries, waterproofing/flooding, roof loads, pool safety and fall protection. A larger plot may be considered if the agreed functions cannot fit; no candidate is automatically approved.
