# Initial Room Schedule (Provisional Targets)

These are target internal dimensions in feet for early visualization. They are not a fitted plan, not net-area guarantees and not code-compliant dimensions. The final schedule must be reconciled against actual plot boundaries, setbacks, wall thicknesses, stairs, bathrooms, circulation, daylight and ventilation.

## Site

- Plot target: 30 ft × 50 ft = 1,500 sq ft gross site area.
- One-car parking target: 9 ft × 16 ft clear rectangle, to be tested against actual vehicle size, gate/access and manoeuvring needs.
- Orientation, road width, setbacks and jurisdiction: unresolved.

## Feasibility warning

Room target areas cannot simply be added and assumed to fit. Shared walls, non-overlapping circulation, parking, staircase, setbacks and required light/ventilation consume space. If the candidate layout is infeasible, report the conflicting constraints and propose options; do not silently resize required rooms.
